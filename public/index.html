Path decisions

Choose a situation

Pick one. Discuss the questions before uncovering a possible direction.

A friend embarrasses you in a group chat

You want to answer immediately.

Discuss: What would leaning only on your own understanding look like? What would trusting the Lord look like?

Pause before replying. Pray, cool down, and choose a soft answer or a private conversation instead of trying to win in public.

You feel pressure to have your future figured out

School, work, a mission, and everything afterward all seem urgent.

Discuss: What do you actually need to decide now? How could the Lord direct one step without showing the whole plan?

Gather good information, pray, seek wise counsel, and act on the best light you have. Trust does not require seeing ten steps ahead.

You know something needs to change

Admitting it feels worse than hiding it.

Discuss: What story might fear tell you? What truth does Jesus Christ offer instead?

Bring the problem into the light. Pray honestly, take responsibility, and ask for appropriate help. Christ offers a way forward, not humiliation.

You prayed and still do not feel certain

You are worried that moving at all means choosing wrong.

Discuss: Can moving forward carefully be an act of trust?

Study the options, keep the commandments, make the wisest choice you can, and move forward. The Lord can redirect someone who is moving.
