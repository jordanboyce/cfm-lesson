Story 1

The Scroll That
Wouldn't Stay Burned

Jeremiah 36

Jeremiah 36 happens before the city falls, while King Jehoiakim is in charge.

💨
🤴🏻
Step 1

Jeremiah speaks the Lord's words. His scribe Baruch writes every one on a scroll.

Jeremiah 36:4

Step 2

The scroll is read to King Jehoiakim. He's sitting in his winter house by a fire.

Jeremiah 36:21–22

Step 3

When Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he cut it with the penknife, and cast it into the fire.Jeremiah 36:23

Step 4

…"until all the roll was consumed in the fire."

And they "were not afraid." (36:23–24)

Your guess

So… what does the Lord do now? 🤔

Give up? Find a new prophet? Something else?

The answer

Take thee again another roll, and write in it all the former words… and there were added besides unto them many like words.Jeremiah 36:28, 32

You can't burn up God's word. 🔥🚫

Click through slowly; each click is one part of the story. On "Your guess," let the class answer before you reveal.

The new scroll had everything the old one had, plus more.

Talk about it

How do people "cut up" God's words today?

🙉 Ignoring them 😂 Making fun of them ✂️ Only keeping the parts we like 📵 "I'll read them… later"

What does it tell you about God that He had the scroll rewritten, with more?

Ask the class to guess the ideas before each one appears.

Point to look for: God is patient and doesn't give up on people. His words keep coming even when people reject them.

Jerusalem's leaders hated Jeremiah's warnings. So they lowered him with ropes into a deep dungeon.

Quick guess

Ebed-melech brought something extra besides ropes. What was it?

Put now these old cast clouts and rotten rags under thine armholes under the cords.Jeremiah 38:12

Why the rags?

So the ropes wouldn't cut into Jeremiah's arms.

Ebed-melech didn't just rescue him. He thought about how the rescue would feel.

Ebed-melech was a foreigner and a servant, but he was the one with the courage to speak up.

Jeremiah 39:15–18

The Lord didn't forget Ebed-melech

I will surely deliver thee… because thou hast put thy trust in me, saith the Lord.Jeremiah 39:18

Who might be "in a pit" around you?

Lonely. Left out. Getting picked on. Having a hard time at home.

Your "old rags" 🧣

Small, thoughtful things that make help hurt less:

🍱 Sit by someone eating alone 📱 "Hey, you OK?" 🛑 Speak up when someone's mocked 🎮 Invite them in 🙏 Pray for them by name

Ask for more "old rags" ideas from the class before revealing the list.

Ebed-melech spoke up when it was risky. Sometimes being kind takes courage.

Lamentations 1

A whole book of sad songs

How doth the city sit solitary, that was full of people!… She weepeth sore in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks.Lamentations 1:1–2

For these things I weep; mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water.Lamentations 1:16

💬 How would you feel if you were there?

Being sad isn't the same as having no faith. God put a whole book of sadness in the scriptures.

But the author doesn't stop at chapter 1… 👉

"Lamentations" means sad poems or cries. Tradition says Jeremiah wrote it.

Let kids talk about the question. It's OK to admit that hard things feel hard.

Lamentations 3:22–24

But then… ☀️

It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. The Lord is my portion… therefore will I hope in him. Lamentations 3:22–24

Every morning = a fresh start from the Lord. 🌅

💬 What's one good thing (a "mercy") you noticed today?

The background just changed from night to sunrise. Point it out: same book, same author, but now he's looking for hope.

Go around the room for the "mercy" question: small stuff counts (breakfast, a friend, sunshine).

Story 4

Buying a Field
in a War Zone

Jeremiah 32

This one happens during the siege, before the city falls. Jeremiah is locked in the prison court.

Would you buy it?

🔒 You're locked up in the palace prison.

⚔️ Babylon's army is right outside the walls.

🧑🏽 Your cousin Hanameel offers to sell you his family field in Anathoth for 17 pieces of silver.

Tap a button for each hand that goes up.

📜
SOLD!

Jeremiah bought it! Signed, sealed, with witnesses, and the deed put in a clay jar to last "many days" (32:9–14).

Houses and fields and vineyards shall be possessed again in this land.Jeremiah 32:15

Ah Lord God!… there is nothing too hard for thee.Jeremiah 32:17

Take the vote first. Then reveal: Jeremiah bought it.

Why? It was a promise you could see: people will live, farm, and buy land here again. He believed God before it happened.

Ask: What's a way you can show you believe good days are coming, even when things look bad?

The big promise

Written in
Your Heart

Jeremiah 31:31–34

This is one of the best-known promises in Jeremiah: a new covenant.

On stone

Love God Love others Be honest Forgive

In your heart

I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people.Jeremiah 31:33

God doesn't just want rules on the outside.
He wants to change who we are on the inside. ❤️

Click 1: the verse. Click 2: the words fly from the stone into the heart. Click 3: the main idea.

Ask: What's the difference between keeping a rule because you have to and because you want to?

Game · Stone or Heart?

Is it just on the outside, or written in the heart?

1 of 6

Going to church only because your parents make you.

That's a start, and it's OK! Heart change takes time. 🌱

2 of 6

Praying because you actually want to talk to Heavenly Father.

That's the new covenant in action. ✨

3 of 6

Being nice to your little brother only when adults are watching.

Real kindness doesn't need an audience. 👀

4 of 6

Telling the truth even when no one would ever find out.

Written in the heart! 💯

5 of 6

Reading one verse just to check it off the list.

Still good! Ask God to help you want it more. 📖

6 of 6

Helping clean up without being asked, just because you love your family.

That's it. That's the whole idea. ❤️

Read each one, have kids point to the stone (left) or the heart (right), then click an answer or press → to reveal.

Keep it gentle: the "outside" ones aren't bad; they're where a lot of us start. The Lord helps move things from outside to inside.

Jeremiah 31

Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.Jeremiah 31:3

I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.Jeremiah 31:34

💬 Why would knowing this make someone want to come back to God?

Remember who first heard these words: people who had messed up badly and lost everything. God still says "everlasting love."

Through Jesus Christ's Atonement, He forgives, and He doesn't keep bringing up our sins.

📞

Call unto me, and I will answer thee, and shew thee great and mighty things, which thou knowest not.Jeremiah 33:3

I will cause the Branch of righteousness to grow up unto David.Jeremiah 33:15

The Branch is Jesus Christ. He's how mourning turns into joy. ✨

Click 1: Jeremiah 33:3, God invites us to call Him. Ask: what's something you could "call" on Him about this week?

Click 2: a new branch grows from a stump. Israel's family tree looked chopped down, but a new Branch would grow from David's family: Jesus Christ.
